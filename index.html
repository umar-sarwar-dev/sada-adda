The Adda

Not a café. An adda.

An adda is what you call the spot where you always end up — not because it's fancy, but because it's yours. Sada Adda started as a stall with two kettles and six stools on a Gulberg side street. It's grown a roof and a menu since, but the stools are still the same six.
